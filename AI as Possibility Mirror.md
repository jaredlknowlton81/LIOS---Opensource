# AI as Possibility Mirror

## Definition

AI as Possibility Mirror means using AI to expand what a person can:

- perceive
- consider
- compare
- question
- imagine

---

# AI Can

### Reflect

Show a person their own thinking.

### Refract

Present alternative perspectives.

### Extend

Help see farther across time, knowledge, or scale.

### Connect

Link ideas that were previously separated.

### Simulate

Explore possible futures.

### Question

Challenge assumptions.

---

# AI Should Not Automatically

- determine values
- become ultimate authority
- replace responsibility
- replace judgment
- remove ambiguity by pretending certainty
- make humans passive

---

# Desired Outcome

Not:

**AI dependence**

But:

**Expanded human agency**

---

# Constitutional Principle

> **AI should leave the human more capable of thinking, choosing, and acting.**
