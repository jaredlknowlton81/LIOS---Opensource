# Knowledge Garden

## Definition

A Knowledge Garden treats knowledge as a living ecology rather than a static archive.

---

# A Garden Requires

- cultivation
- connection
- revision
- pruning
- cross-pollination
- experimentation
- preservation
- regeneration

---

# Knowledge as Living Matter

Ideas can:

- grow
- combine
- conflict
- mutate
- become obsolete
- generate new ideas

Therefore knowledge systems should not merely store information.

They should create conditions for emergence.

---

# Relationship to Obsidian

Obsidian itself can function as a Knowledge Garden.

Individual notes are not the intelligence.

The intelligence emerges through:

**Notes → Links → Patterns → Models → Questions → New Notes**
