# Community World Model

A Community World Model is the shared model through which a community interprets reality.

---

# Components

- observations
- assumptions
- stories
- values
- history
- relationships
- causal models
- expectations
- possible futures

---

# Why It Matters

People do not coordinate around raw reality.

They coordinate around:

> **What they believe reality means.**

Therefore collective intelligence depends heavily on the quality of the shared world model.

---

# Healthy World Models

A mature world model:

- distinguishes facts from interpretations
- acknowledges uncertainty
- welcomes correction
- allows multiple perspectives
- updates when reality changes
- preserves useful memory
