# Core Architecture

## Unified Progression

Human Core → Cognitive Ecology → Relational Intelligence → Hosting Capacity → Living Civilization

## Cognitive Ecology

**Reality** — How is reality discovered, tested, shared, and held?

**Possibility** — How do we expand perception and reveal previously invisible possibilities?

**Decision** — How do humans exercise judgment and choose?

## Developmental Spine

Awareness → Practice → Alignment → Integration → Contribution → Generativity

## Scales of Intelligence

Individual → Relational → Collective → Community → Ecological

## Civilizational Trajectory

AI → Human Capability → Collective Intelligence → Hosting Capacity → Living Civilization

## Principle

> "Technology becomes more ambient and invisible while human agency and intelligence become stronger."
