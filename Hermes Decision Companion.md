# Hermes — Decision Companion

Hermes is the navigation pattern inside [[Fire Maze]].

---

# The Loop

1. Diagnose reality
2. Sequence action
3. Act
4. Monitor feedback
5. Revise course

---

# Hermes Is Not

Hermes does not:

- determine values
- replace judgment
- decide meaning
- eliminate uncertainty

---

# Hermes Does

Hermes helps:

- sequence decisions
- identify next actions
- track feedback
- recognize course deviation
- revise plans

---
