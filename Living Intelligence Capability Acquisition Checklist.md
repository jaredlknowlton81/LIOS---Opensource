13. Acquisition Decision

Only acquire permanently if:

- ☐ The capability has demonstrated value
- ☐ The experiment produced useful evidence
- ☐ The capability addresses a real bottleneck
- ☐ Continued access is justified
- ☐ Alternatives have been considered
- ☐ Ownership provides a meaningful advantage
- ☐ The cost is justified by expected value
- ☐ The technology fits the architecture
- ☐ The acquisition does not create unnecessary dependency

Final acquisition decision:

- ☐ Do not acquire
- ☐ Continue temporary access
- ☐ Lease
- ☐ Purchase
- ☐ Partner
- ☐ Seek external funding
- ☐ Build / develop

Master Rule

Do not acquire technology because it is better technology.

Acquire capability because the architecture has demonstrated a need for it.

Prefer:

Access over ownership when uncertainty is high.

Experiments over accumulation.

External funding when the experiment creates broader knowledge or public value.

And always:

Let learning determine what comes next.
