# Observatory

The Observatory is the temporal and systemic perception layer.

It asks:

> What patterns become visible when we watch long enough?

---

# It Watches

- trends
- cycles
- changes
- anomalies
- feedback
- relationships
- weak signals
- repeated behaviors

---

# Difference from Listening Post

**Listening Post = receiving signals**

**Observatory = interpreting patterns across time**

---

# Archetype

The Observatory is the intelligence function of:

> **Pattern recognition.**
