- decisions

Fundamental question

How did we come to believe this?

The Ledger should eventually make it possible to answer:

- What do we believe?
- Why?
- Based on what?
- Who introduced the claim?
- What challenged it?
- What changed our confidence?
- What did we do because of it?
- What happened afterward?

23. Architectural Principles

24. Experience before abstraction

Reality enters before interpretation.

2. Memory must preserve relationships

Context matters as much as individual facts.

3. Provenance matters

Every important claim should be traceable.

4. Disagreement is data

Do not erase competing interpretations.

5. Uncertainty is information

Confidence should be represented explicitly.

6. AI should challenge as well as assist

A useful intelligence system needs epistemic friction.

7. Humans retain agency

AI supports judgment rather than silently replacing it.

8. Action creates knowledge

The world is part of the learning system.

9. Feedback closes the loop

Without consequences, learning remains theoretical.

10. Intelligence compounds through persistent context

Memory allows today’s experience to influence tomorrow’s reasoning.

  

24. The Central Question

How do we build an environment in which humans and AI can continuously learn from reality without losing provenance, uncertainty, disagreement, agency, or memory?
