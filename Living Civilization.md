# Living Civilization

Living Civilization is the civilization-scale expression of Living Intelligence.

---

# Definition

A Living Civilization is not simply a technologically advanced civilization.

It is a civilization capable of:

- sensing
- learning
- adapting
- remembering
- regenerating
- creating
- contributing

while preserving meaningful human agency and freedom.

---

# Emergence

**People → Relationships → Practices → Culture → Structures → Feedback**

Civilization becomes living when these layers can continuously learn together.

---

# Scale

**Individual → Relationship → Community → Society → Civilization**

---

# Intelligence Archetypes at Civilization Scale

### Mirror

Civilization examines itself.

### Observatory

Civilization observes long-term patterns.

### Prism

Civilization sees multiple perspectives.

### Telescope

Civilization sees across generations and systems.

### Compass

Civilization establishes direction.

### Garden

Civilization cultivates future possibility.

---

# Civilization and Freedom

A civilization can become extremely efficient while reducing human freedom.

Therefore efficiency is not the final metric.

The deeper question is:

> **Does civilization increase meaningful freedom and the capacity to contribute?**

---

# Generativity

The mature civilization does not merely preserve itself.

It creates conditions in which future people can:

- develop
- choose
- contribute
- create
- flourish
- create conditions for others
