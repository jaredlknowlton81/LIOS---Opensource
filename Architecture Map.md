## The Meta-Architecture

The important insight is that these are not competing frameworks.

They are different views of the same living system.

The architecture is therefore better understood as a:

**Generative Grammar**

rather than a single rigid model.

---

## Constitutional Question

> Does the architecture make people more capable of freely choosing meaningful lives and creating worlds in which others can do the same?
