# 16 — Staff Engineer

**Book:** *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track*  
**Author:** Will Larson

## Role in the Architecture

**Distributed technical leadership**

The staff engineer concept is useful as a model for influence without requiring formal managerial authority.

## Living Intelligence Connection

Individual technical expertise can propagate through relationships, decisions, architecture, and organizational coordination.

**Individual intelligence → distributed influence → organizational capability**

## Questions

- Where does expertise reside?
- How does expertise move through the organization?
- How is technical judgment preserved?
- How can leadership operate through networks rather than hierarchy?
