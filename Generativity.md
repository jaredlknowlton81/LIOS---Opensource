# Generativity

Generativity is the ability to create conditions in which others can become capable, free, and contributive.

---

# Purpose Sequence

**Perception → Agency → Freedom → Contribution → Generativity**

---

# Difference Between Contribution and Generativity

Contribution asks:

> What can I create?

Generativity asks:

> What can I create that helps others create?

---

# Civilization

A generative civilization does not merely preserve itself.

It creates conditions for future generations to:

- learn
- choose
- create
- contribute
- flourish

---

# Deep Principle

> **The highest form of contribution may be creating the conditions for other people's agency.**
