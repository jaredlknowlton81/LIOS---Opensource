# 17 — Scaling People

**Book:** *Scaling People: Tactics for Management and Company Building*  
**Author:** Claire Hughes Johnson

## Role in the Architecture

**Organizational learning and scaling**

The central relevance to Living Intelligence is the creation of mechanisms through which individual capability can scale into organizational capability.

## Living Intelligence Connection

**Individual → Team → Organization**

Systems for management, development, communication, and decision-making can become organizational learning mechanisms.

## Questions

- How does an organization preserve learning?
- How does individual development become organizational capability?
- How do management systems adapt as complexity increases?
