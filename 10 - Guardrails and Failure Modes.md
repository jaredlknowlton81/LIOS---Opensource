# Guardrails and Failure Modes

## Permanent Guardrails

**Reality over Narrative**
Don't let the system believe its own story.

**Human Agency over AI Authority**
AI informs judgment; it does not own judgment.

**Meaning over Optimization**
Metrics serve human purposes.

**Dissent over Conformity**
Healthy systems make disagreement possible.

**Adaptability over Institutional Permanence**
Hosting must preserve the ability to change.

## Failure Checklist

Before scaling an intervention, ask:

- Are we reflecting more than acting?
- Have metrics become goals?
- Is AI becoming an authority?
- Are possibilities preventing decisions?
- Is trust suppressing dissent?
- Is infrastructure becoming bureaucracy?
- Are we assuming everyone wants optimization?
- Who is excluded?
- Are we becoming an echo chamber?
- Are we exaggerating the scale of transformation?
- Can the system handle failure, grief, and irreversible loss?
- What happens when the system is wrong?

## Design Principle

A robust Living Intelligence system should improve its ability to:

Detect Error → Surface Disagreement → Learn → Adapt → Preserve Meaning → Continue
