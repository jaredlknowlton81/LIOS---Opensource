# Where to Walk

## Environmental Development

Different environments cultivate different:

- emotional states
- perceptions
- questions
- possibilities
- capabilities

## Core Proposition

> "Environment shapes how we feel. Repeated practice within environments shapes who we become."

## Portfolio

- Restoration
- Curiosity
- Connection
- Reflection
- Challenge
- Sustainable Practice
- Contribution

## Relationship to Possibility

Deliberately varied environments can make new perceptions and possibilities visible.

The environment does not provide the answer. It changes the conditions under which people can perceive and act.

## Community Implication

Design a portfolio of environments rather than one generic space.

> "A place can become a developmental curriculum without looking like a school."
