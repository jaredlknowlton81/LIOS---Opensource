# Scenario Engine

The Scenario Engine is the possibility-exploration layer.

---

# Core Questions

- What could happen?
- What else could happen?
- What assumptions are we making?
- What happens if an assumption changes?
- What paths are available?
- What are the consequences?
- What signals would tell us we are wrong?

---

# Function

The Scenario Engine expands:

**Possibility**

without confusing possibility with prediction.

---

# Relationship to Decision

A narrow possibility space creates narrow decisions.

A broader possibility space creates more potential agency.

Therefore:

**Possibility expansion → Choice expansion → Agency expansion**
