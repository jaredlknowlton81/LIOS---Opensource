# Mirror · Observatory · Prism · Telescope · Compass · Garden

These six archetypes describe recurring intelligence functions.

---

# Mirror

## Function

Reflection.

Questions:

- What am I assuming?
- What am I missing?
- What does my own thinking reveal?

---

# Observatory

## Function

Pattern recognition.

Questions:

- What is changing?
- What repeats?
- What patterns appear over time?

---

# Prism

## Function

Perspective expansion.

Questions:

- How else could this be understood?
- What would another person see?
- What assumptions change the interpretation?

---

# Telescope

## Function

Extended perception.

Questions:

- What happens at a larger scale?
- What happens over a longer period?
- What precedents or distant signals matter?

---

# Compass

## Function

Orientation.

Questions:

- Where are we going?
- What matters?
- Which direction aligns with our values?

---

# Garden

## Function

Cultivation.

Questions:

- What needs nurturing?
- What conditions support growth?
- What should be connected?
- What should be pruned?
- What might emerge?

---

# The Six Together

```text
MIRROR
   ↓
OBSERVATORY
   ↓
PRISM
   ↓
TELESCOPE
   ↓
COMPASS
   ↓
GARDEN
```

They are not necessarily six products.

They are six ways an environment can increase intelligence.
