# Listening Post

## Purpose

The Listening Post is the perception-before-interpretation environment.

Its purpose is to receive reality before rushing to explain it.

---

# Practices

- listen
- observe
- collect signals
- notice weak signals
- suspend premature certainty
- separate observation from interpretation
- notice what does not fit the existing model

---

# Why It Matters

If perception is distorted, everything downstream is distorted.

Therefore:

**Bad input → bad model → bad possibility space → bad decision**

The Listening Post protects the beginning of the intelligence cycle.
