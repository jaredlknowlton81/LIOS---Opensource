# Reality → Possibility → Decision

This is the core cognitive cycle.

---

# Reality

Ask:

> What is actually happening?

This requires observation.

---

# Possibility

Ask:

> What could happen?

> What alternatives exist?

> What assumptions could change?

---

# Decision

Ask:

> What should we choose?

This is where:

- values
- responsibility
- constraints
- judgment
- priorities

enter.

---

# Full Loop

```text
REALITY
   ↓
POSSIBILITY
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
PRACTICE
   ↓
FEEDBACK
   ↓
REVISED REALITY
```

---

# Important

AI can assist with:

Reality + Possibility

Humans must retain:

Judgment + Responsibility
