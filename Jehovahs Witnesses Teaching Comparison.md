# Jehovah's Witnesses Teaching Approach — Structural Comparison

## The Comparison

There is a structural similarity between Living Intelligence and the teaching approach associated with Jehovah's Witnesses as presented through jw.org.

---

# Structural Similarity

Both can be understood as emphasizing a movement from:

**Listening → Understanding → Application → Wise Action**

The similarity is primarily about the structure of learning and application.

---

# Fundamental Difference

The authority structure is different.

In the Living Intelligence architecture:

- AI assists perception
- AI assists reflection
- humans retain judgment
- humans retain responsibility
- humans retain agency
- technology is not the ultimate authority

The framework therefore uses:

> **AI-assisted perception and human judgment**

rather than Scripture as the ultimate source of authority.

---

# Why the Comparison Matters

It raises a broader question:

> **How does a system move a person from information toward wise action?**

Living Intelligence answers through:

**Perception → Understanding → Possibility → Judgment → Practice → Feedback**

---

# The Key Difference

The Living Intelligence approach is not primarily an authority system.

It is a:

> **Perception + Agency + Practice + Feedback system.**
