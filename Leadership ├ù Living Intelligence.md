## Key Insight

Management is not merely a wrapper around technology.

It can be one of the mechanisms through which a system:

- acquires experience
- preserves context
- distributes intelligence
- develops capability
- generates feedback
- learns
- adapts

## Acquisition Implication

A capability should be assessed partly by whether it strengthens these human and organizational learning loops.
