# Character → Human Operating System → Community World Model → Knowledge Structures → Living Intelligence

This is the developmental spine of the architecture.

---

# 1. Character

The person develops:

- integrity
- courage
- humility
- curiosity
- responsibility
- resilience
- reflection

Character answers:

> **Who am I becoming?**

---

# 2. Human Operating System

The person develops practices for:

- attention
- perception
- learning
- sensemaking
- decision
- action
- reflection
- relationships

This answers:

> **How do I operate?**

---

# 3. Community World Model

People begin to construct shared understanding.

Questions include:

- What is happening?
- What matters?
- What do we believe?
- What don't we know?
- What possibilities exist?
- How are our actions connected?

This answers:

> **How do we understand our world together?**

---

# 4. Knowledge Structures

The community learns to preserve intelligence.

Through:

- stories
- language
- documentation
- practices
- rituals
- institutions
- memory
- feedback

This answers:

> **How does intelligence persist?**

---

# 5. Living Intelligence

The whole ecology becomes capable of:

**Sense → Interpret → Imagine → Decide → Act → Learn → Adapt → Regenerate**

This answers:

> **How does the living system become capable of learning?**

---

# Critical Insight

The endpoint is not:

> Smarter individuals.

The endpoint is:

> **More intelligent relationships and systems.**
