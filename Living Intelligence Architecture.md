# Living Intelligence Architecture

## Core Model

Agents / Entities  
↓  
Relationships  
↓  
Interaction / Experience  
↓  
Capture  
↓  
Persistent Context / Ledger  
↓  
AI + Human Sensemaking  
↓  
Knowledge  
↓  
Action  
↓  
Feedback  
↓  
Learning  
↓  
Adaptation  
↓  
Emergence  
↓  
Network Intelligence  
↓  
Planetary Intelligence

## Key Idea

Living Intelligence is not simply the automation of a task. It is the capacity of a system to accumulate experience, preserve context, make sense of that experience, act, receive feedback, learn, and adapt.
