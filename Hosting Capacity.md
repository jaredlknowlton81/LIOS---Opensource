# Hosting Capacity

Hosting Capacity is the ability of a person, relationship, community, or institution to sustain intelligence over time.

---

# What Intelligence Requires

Intelligence requires enough:

- time
- attention
- resources
- trust
- stability
- memory
- continuity
- relationships

to remain active.

---

# Fire Maze

---

# Why It Matters

A brilliant idea cannot become living intelligence if there is no capacity to:

- practice it
- preserve it
- transmit it
- adapt it
- support the people carrying it

---

# Insight

> **Intelligence needs somewhere to live.**

Hosting Capacity is the infrastructure that allows intelligence to persist.
