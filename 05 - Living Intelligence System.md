# Living Intelligence System

## More Than a Second Brain

The system becomes: **Mirror + Partner + Living Environment for Thinking**

## Operational Loop

You → Knowledge → Reasoning → Living Intelligence → You

## Knowledge Lifecycle

Capture → Process → Connect → Use → Reflect → Improve

## Core Questions

1. What do I know?
2. What don't I know?
3. What have I forgotten?
4. What ideas are connected?
5. Where is my thinking contradictory?
6. Which questions keep recurring?
7. What is my knowledge becoming?

The seventh question introduces trajectory.

## Evolution

AI as Tool → AI as Partner → Coupled System → Emergent Intelligence → Living Intelligence

## Expansion

Individual Intelligence → Relational Intelligence → Community Intelligence → Hosting Capacity → Living Civilization

## Distinction

> "A second brain stores."

> "A thinking partner helps reason."

> "A living environment changes what is possible for the person inside it."
