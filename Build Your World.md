# Build Your World

Build Your World is a design metaphor for Living Intelligence.

---

# Central Idea

Instead of building one monolithic system:

> Build an ecology of environments.

Each environment helps people develop a different capability.

---

# Possible Rooms

## Listening Post

Receive reality.

## Observatory

Notice patterns.

## Knowledge Garden

Cultivate knowledge.

## Scenario Engine

Explore possibilities.

## Practice Space

Develop skills.

## Reflection Space

Examine assumptions.

## Community Space

Coordinate with others.

## Compass Space

Choose direction.

---

# The World as Ecology

Each environment can have:

- different rules
- different practices
- different relationships
- different rhythms
- different forms of knowledge

But the environments connect.

---

# Core Principle

> **Build environments that make better ways of seeing, relating, practicing, deciding, and contributing easier to inhabit.**
