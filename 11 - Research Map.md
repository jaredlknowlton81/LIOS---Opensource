## Emerging Architecture

1. Reality is continuously checked.
2. Possibility is expanded but eventually narrowed.
3. Humans retain decision authority.
4. Practice creates real-world feedback.
5. Reflection turns experience into learning.
6. Communities share and compound learning.
7. Hosting preserves learning without freezing it.
8. AI remains ambient infrastructure.
9. Meaning and values constrain optimization.
10. The system remains capable of discovering that it is wrong.

## Emerging Thesis

> "Living Intelligence is an adaptive human-AI ecology that continuously senses reality, expands possibility, supports human judgment, turns decisions into practice, learns from feedback, strengthens relationships, builds community intelligence, and creates the hosting capacity required for that intelligence to persist and compound."
