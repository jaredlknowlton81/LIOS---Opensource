# Living Intelligence Operating Model

## Central Loop

Sense → Understand → Expand → Decide → Practice → Reflect → Adapt → Contribute

## Seven Operational Questions

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Reality | What is actually happening? |
| Possibility | What else could be possible? |
| Decision | What do we choose? |
| Practice | What will we do repeatedly? |
| Reflection | What are we learning? |
| Community | Who are we becoming together? |
| Hosting | What allows intelligence to persist and compound? |

## Overarching Question

> "What are we becoming?"

## Weekly Rhythm

**Monday — Sense**
Capture observations and conditions.

**Tuesday — Explore**
Expand perspectives and possibilities.

**Wednesday — Decide**
Clarify tradeoffs and choose.

**Thursday–Friday — Practice**
Run the experiment.

**Weekend — Reflect**
Review evidence, experience, surprises, and outcomes.

**Monthly — Connect**
Identify patterns across experiences and relationships.

**Quarterly — Rebuild**
Assess whether environment, community, and infrastructure support desired development.

## Developmental Progression

Awareness → Practice → Alignment → Integration → Contribution → Generativity

## Scale Progression

Individual → Relational → Collective → Community → Ecological
