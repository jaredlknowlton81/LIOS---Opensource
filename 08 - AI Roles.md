# AI Roles

AI is connective tissue throughout the Living Intelligence ecosystem.

## Mirror

Reflects patterns in:

- thinking
- behavior
- knowledge
- experience

## Prism

Introduces:

- alternative perspectives
- interpretations
- analogies
- counterexamples

## Telescope

Extends perception toward:

- possibilities
- scenarios
- futures

## Observatory

Detects patterns across:

- information
- experience
- behavior
- community learning

## Compass

Supports navigation among options without owning the decision.

## Memory

Preserves continuity and makes prior learning available.

## Coordinator

Reduces administrative friction and supports collective action.

## Boundary

AI should not replace:

- human values
- relationships
- empathy
- wisdom
- moral disagreement
- celebration
- tradition
- human agency

## Decision Boundary

Observation → Inference → Recommendation → Decision

AI supports the first three. Humans/community own the decision.
