# Living Intelligence — Personal to Planetary Architecture

> The continuous synthesis of self, relationships, world-models, memory, learning, adaptation, and technological capability.

## North Star

Living Intelligence is the capacity of an entity or network to continuously:

**Sense → Relate → Remember → Make Sense → Learn → Adapt → Act → Reconfigure**


The intelligence is not contained entirely within an individual agent.

It emerges through the persistence of relationships and context across time.
